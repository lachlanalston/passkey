Passkey Lab

See how passkeys actually work — by making one, using one, and watching what happens underneath. Nothing leaves this browser.

Guided lab

Six steps · about 10 minutes

  • Make a real passkey
  • Sign in with it, timed against a texted code
  • Do it again from your phone, over QR
  • Break it on purpose, so you know the failure
  • Try to phish yourself, and watch it get nothing
  • Clean up, and prove it's gone

Every step ends with an X-ray of what actually happened — and where the same thing shows up in Microsoft Entra.

The bench

  • Five demos of why passkeys hold
  • A ledger you can export and import

Nothing is in order and nothing is gated. Start wherever you like.

The three pillars
  • A lookalike domain asks the browser for your passkey.
  • One byte of a genuine login is altered in transit.
  • A real, valid login is captured and sent a second time.
Extra checks
  • The signature is checked against somebody else's public key.
  • Did they just touch it, or prove who they are?

All five need a passkey made on this site to work with — create one in step 1 first.
